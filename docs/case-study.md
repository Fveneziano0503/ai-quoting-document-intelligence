# Case study

## Objective
Make quoting assumptions and missing RFQ information visible before a pricing decision.

## Approach
Separate information capture, technical review, costing, and release. Apply manufacturing experience to distinguish recurring unit costs from setup costs and gross margin from markup. Keep a human review gate before export.

## Builder contribution
Federico defined the operational workflow and manufacturing quoting requirements. This public prototype implements a limited text parser and manual cost model to demonstrate the review process.

## Measurement plan
Compare time to prepare a reviewed quote, clarification count, missing-revision rate, and estimate-to-actual cost variance against an established baseline. No performance results are claimed here.
